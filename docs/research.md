# Reference research (2026-09-24)

Scraped with Apify `apify/rag-web-browser` (home pages only):

| Site | What we took |
|---|---|
| Egon Zehnder | One short human statement as the hero ("Leadership matters. Let's get it right.") instead of a busy slider; restraint over volume. |
| Heidrick & Struggles | A signature line ("One leadership team at a time") and a visible **recruitment-scam notice** for candidates. |
| Robert Walters Middle East | A permanent **two-audience split** ("Looking to hire" / "Register your CV") and sector cards with one-line descriptions. |
| Cooper Fitch (Dubai) | Page returned no content; not used. |

What we deliberately did *not* copy: client-logo walls, testimonials, headcount/office counters and
salary calculators. They need real evidence, which NUUK & Human does not have yet (brief rule: no
fabricated clients, results or statistics).

## Resulting design choices

- Dark navy hero taken from the logo (#0C1223) with the NUUKquant accent blue (#0165FA) and cyan (#00D3F3).
- The logo's ampersand is the recurring graphic motif (hero, page heads, closing CTA) instead of stock photos.
- Serif display (Cormorant Garamond, close to the logo) paired with Manrope (the NUUKquant body face).
- Employer / candidate split cards overlap the hero directly under the fold.
- Candidate page leads with the no-fee promise and the scam notice.
